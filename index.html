Where policy meets what’s next.

I spent a decade in the rooms where policy gets made, from UN summits to Parliament Hill. Now I bring that public-affairs instinct to innovation, health research and technology: helping the people building what’s next earn the attention, trust and support of the people who decide what happens next.

02 About
“I have a knack for transforming complex, technical concepts into compelling, accessible messages that resonate with diverse audiences.”

I am a multilingual, international communications expert with over 15 years of experience in crafting data-driven messaging strategies and managing media relations for policy outcomes across a wide range of sectors, including science, technology, innovation, and research.

With a strong focus on using data and insights to shape narratives, I have supported global communications campaigns that drive political and social change.

Throughout my career, I have been responsible for implementing proactive and reactive strategies, ranging from narrative cohesion and messaging development to rapid response and crisis communications. I am a passionate storyteller, critical thinker, project manager, and problem solver.
