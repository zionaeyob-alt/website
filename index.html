02 — About

“I have a knack for transforming complex, technical concepts into compelling, accessible messages that resonate with diverse audiences.”

I am a multilingual, international communications expert with over 15 years of experience in crafting data-driven messaging strategies and managing media relations for policy outcomes across a wide range of sectors, including science, technology, innovation, and research. With a strong focus on using data and insights to shape narratives, I have supported global communications campaigns that drive political and social change.
