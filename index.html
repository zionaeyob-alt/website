01 About
“I have a knack for transforming complex, technical concepts into compelling, accessible messages that resonate with diverse audiences.”

I’m a multilingual, international communications strategist with more than fifteen years of experience at the intersection of policy, innovation and public affairs, across science, technology, research and climate.

I’ve briefed ministers on the floor of UN climate summits, led national media strategy for Canada’s environmental movement, and today help position Canada as a global innovation leader at Mitacs, engaging policymakers on innovation, technology and AI.

Whether it’s narrative development, a coalition campaign or a rapid-response moment, I lead with data and insight, and I write for the person on the other side of the page.
